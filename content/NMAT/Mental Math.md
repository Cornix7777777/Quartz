
# Divide by 5
Multiply non-zero no. by 2
- if no zero add decimal point
- if zero in-between, remains a zero
- When number is over ten when doubled, pair numbers together, then times 2
  ![[Pasted image 20261103161307.png]]![[Pasted image 20261103161452.png]]
- And also use common sense (ie. 3600/5 cannot be 72, thus 720)
- When excess third number, round off/estimate to number easily divisible by 5, and then add/subtract missing/excess to final answer
