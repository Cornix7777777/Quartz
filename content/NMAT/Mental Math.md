# Divide by 5
Multiply non-zero numbers by 2
- If no zero add decimal point
- If zero in-between, remains a zero
- When number ids over 10 when doubled, pair numbers together, then times 2
![[Pasted image 20261103191818.png]]![[Pasted image 20261103191825.png]]
- Also use common sense (ie. 3600/5 cannot be 72, thus must be 720)
- When excess third number, round off/estimate to number easily divisible by 5, and then add/subtract missing/excess to final answer
